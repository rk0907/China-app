How it works

Five simple steps from request to delivery in Ghana.

01

Tell us what you need

Submit an item description, image or supplier/product link. Include quantity, budget and any preferences.

02

We source it

Our team searches Chinese suppliers for suitable options that match your brief and budget.

03

You receive a quotation

We calculate the estimated landed price in Ghana Cedis and send you clear options.

04

Approve & pay

Choose your preferred option and make payment according to the instructions we provide. No payment before you have a quotation.

05

We handle procurement

We purchase, consolidate, ship and update you until your order reaches Ghana and is ready for delivery.
